Privacy policy

Effective [DATE]

Nihlus Vision ([LEGAL COMPANY NAME, e.g. Nihlus Vision LLC], “Nihlus,” “we,” “us”) provides cargo security: verified driver pickup, locked destinations, trailer monitoring and incident response. This policy explains what personal information we collect through our website, our apps and our trailer devices, how we use and share it, and the choices you have.

Most of our service is provided to businesses such as shippers, carriers and brokers (“Customers”). When a Customer uses Nihlus for its loads, it decides which drivers, trailers and shipments are monitored. If you’re a driver, you may also want to read your carrier’s or employer’s own policies.

1. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Contact and business details you submit through our website forms, such as your name, company, work email, plan of interest, fleet size and the cargo or lanes you want to protect.
  • Investor inquiries, such as your name, firm, email, investor type, whether you are an accredited investor and your message.
  • Customer account information, such as user names, work contact details and login credentials for the command center.
  • Load information our Customers provide, such as bills of lading, seal and trailer numbers, pickup windows, destinations and approved carrier rosters.

Information from drivers using the Nihlus app

  • Identity details, such as name, carrier and commercial driver’s license (CDL) information.
  • Biometric information used to verify the driver at pickup, but only after the driver gives consent (see Biometric information).
  • Consent records, including when consent was given or withdrawn.
  • Location from the driver’s phone [DECISION: only at pickup / during an active load / other].
  • Messages and alerts sent through the app, including attempted reroute messages we block.
  • Device information, such as phone model, operating system and app version.

Information from Nihlus trailer devices

  • Location and route data, including GPS position, speed and geofence entries and exits.
  • Camera images and video from the trailer door camera. Photos are captured when an event is detected. Live video is captured only when an alert fires or an authorized user taps in for live evidence. Images may show people who are near the trailer.
  • Device and event data, such as door, tamper, power, battery and signal status.

Information created when an incident happens

  • Incident timelines and evidence packages, which may combine photos, video clips, GPS trails, load documents, pickup records and actions taken.

2. Biometric information

To confirm that the right driver picks up a load, the Nihlus app may compare the driver’s face [and iris, if this feature is offered] with the driver’s enrolled profile and license.

  • Consent first. We collect biometric information only after the driver agrees in the app. The consent is timestamped and saved with the pickup record.
  • One purpose. We use biometric information only to verify the driver’s identity for pickups.
  • No selling. We never sell, lease or trade biometric information, and we don’t use it for advertising.
  • Limited sharing. We share biometric information only with service providers who process it for us under contract [name the verification vendor, if any], or when the law requires it.

3. How we use information

  • To provide the service: verify drivers, lock destinations, monitor routes, detect threats and respond to incidents.
  • To block shipment-misdirection attempts, such as spoofed emails, texts or calls, and to keep evidence of them.
  • To build evidence packages for our Customers, law enforcement and insurers when an incident occurs.
  • To respond to pilot requests, investor inquiries and support questions.
  • To maintain, secure and improve our devices, apps and service.
  • To comply with the law and enforce our agreements.

We don’t sell personal information, and we don’t use it for targeted advertising.

4. How we share information

  • With our Customers. The shipper, carrier or broker responsible for a load can see information about that load, its driver verification and any incidents.
  • With law enforcement and insurers, when an incident occurs and the Customer approves the action, or when the law requires it.
  • With service providers that help us run Nihlus, such as cloud hosting, cellular connectivity, website hosting and form delivery. They may use the information only to provide services to us.
  • For legal reasons, such as responding to lawful requests or protecting the rights, property or safety of Nihlus, our Customers, drivers or others.
  • In a business transfer, such as a merger or acquisition, subject to this policy.

6. How we protect it

We use administrative, technical and physical safeguards designed to protect personal information. These include encryption in transit, access controls and signed command-center instructions. No system is perfectly secure, so we can’t guarantee absolute security.

7. Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • know what personal information we hold about you and get a copy
  • correct inaccurate information
  • delete your information
  • withdraw consent, including biometric consent
  • appeal if we deny your request

To make a request, contact us using the details below. We’ll verify your request and respond within the time the law requires. If your information was collected for a Customer, such as your carrier, we may forward your request to that Customer or ask you to contact them. We won’t discriminate against you for exercising your rights.

8. Website, cookies and forms

  • Our website doesn’t use advertising or analytics cookies.
  • Our site is hosted by Vercel, which keeps standard server logs, such as IP address, browser type and pages requested, for security and operations.
  • We load fonts from Google Fonts, so your browser connects to Google when you visit.
  • Forms on our site are delivered to us by Web3Forms, a form-handling service.

9. Children

Nihlus is a business service and isn’t directed to children. We don’t knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as our service changes. We’ll post the new version here with a new effective date. If we make material changes, we’ll take additional steps to let you know, such as notifying our Customers or showing a notice in the app.
