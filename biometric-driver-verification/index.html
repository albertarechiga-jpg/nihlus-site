Biometric pickup · Driver identity

The right driver, or no pickup.

Nihlus Vision verifies the driver before the doors close. With the driver’s consent, iris, face and CDL are checked on the driver’s own phone, then matched to the carrier, BOL, seal and trailer on the shipper’s release. The shipper releases the load only to the verified driver.

Why the pickup is where theft starts

The most expensive cargo thefts today don’t involve a break-in. They start with someone who looks like the right carrier showing up at the dock with paperwork that looks right. Once the doors close and the truck leaves, tracking can only tell you where the load went.

Nihlus Vision moves the check to the one moment you control: before the load is released. If the driver, the carrier or the paperwork doesn’t match, the pickup doesn’t happen.

How a verified pickup works

  1. Driver checks in

    The driver opens the Nihlus app at the dock. The shipper’s terminal waits for a verified driver.

  2. Driver gives consent

    Before any scan, the driver agrees to biometric verification on the phone. The consent is timestamped and saved with the pickup record.

  3. Iris scan

    The phone scans the driver’s iris and matches it to the enrolled driver profile.

  4. Face and license

    A face check and the driver’s CDL confirm it’s the same person.

  5. Nihlus AI cross-check

    Nihlus Vision checks the carrier roster, BOL, seal, trailer and pickup window against the shipper’s release.

  6. Visual release to the shipper

    When every check passes, the shipper sees the verified driver’s photo and checks on the dock terminal, then releases the load.

  7. Driver cleared to go

    The driver’s phone turns green with the locked destination, and the door unit arms for the trip.

What gets checked before release

The person

Driver consent on file, iris match and face match on the driver’s phone.

The credentials

A valid CDL, and a carrier that’s on your approved roster.

The load

BOL, seal and trailer match the shipper’s release, inside the scheduled pickup window.

Consent and privacy

Biometric verification starts only after the driver agrees to it on the phone. Nihlus uses the driver’s iris and face only to confirm the driver for that pickup, and the consent is recorded with a timestamp and saved with the pickup record.

The start of an unbroken record

A verified pickup is the first link in the Nihlus custody chain. The destination is locked at pickup so a fake BOL can’t reroute the truck. The route is geofenced, and a camera on the trailer door watches every mile. If something goes wrong, the pickup record, door photos, GPS trail and BOL are packaged as evidence for law enforcement and your insurer.

Questions

Does the driver need special hardware?

No. Verification runs in the Nihlus app on the driver’s phone. The shipper sees the result on the dock terminal.

What if a check fails?

The load isn’t released. The shipper releases only to a driver who passes every check.

Is the driver’s consent required?

Yes. The driver agrees to biometric verification before any scan, and that consent is timestamped and saved with the pickup record.

What else is matched besides the driver?

The carrier roster, BOL, seal, trailer and pickup window are all checked against the shipper’s release.
