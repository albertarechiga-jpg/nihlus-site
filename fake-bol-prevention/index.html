How a fake BOL steals a load

Shipment misdirection doesn’t look like theft while it’s happening. The truck, the driver and the paperwork are all real. Only the destination is wrong.

The spoofed email

An “urgent updated BOL” with a new drop address arrives from a lookalike domain, one letter off from a carrier or broker you trust.

The call to the driver

Someone posing as dispatch calls or texts the driver directly with a new address, skipping your office entirely.

The real driver, the wrong dock

The driver follows instructions that look legitimate and delivers the load to the thieves. GPS tracking shows exactly where it went, after it’s gone.

Tracking tells you where it went. Nihlus stops it from going.

A GPS tracker reports the truck’s position, but it can’t tell a legitimate reroute from a fraudulent one. Nihlus Vision changes who is allowed to move the load in the first place.

  1. Destination locked at pickup

    Once the driver is verified and the load is released, the drop address is locked and signed by your command center.

  2. Spoofed email rejected

    An emailed BOL with a new drop address from an unverified sender is rejected. The destination doesn’t change, and the sender and message are saved as evidence.

  3. Call or text to the driver blocked

    When a caller posing as dispatch texts the driver a new address, the Nihlus app blocks it and reports it.

  4. Repeat attempts blocked fleet-wide

    If the same lookalike sender tries again, it is blocked across your whole fleet, not just one truck.

  5. Real reroutes still work

    When your manager changes the drop in the command center, the change is signed, approved and reaches the driver. Your team keeps full control.

If a load does leave its route

Destination lock works alongside the rest of the Nihlus custody chain. Every route is geofenced, so a truck leaving its approved corridor raises an alert to your manager and Nihlus AI at the same moment. The incident gets a running clock, Nihlus AI recommends the next actions in order, and law enforcement dispatch is one tap away.

Photos, live evidence clips, the GPS trail, the BOL and the pickup record are packaged automatically, so your insurer receives the full record with the claim.

Questions

Can the driver change the destination?

No. After pickup, only your command center can change the drop address. Instructions sent straight to the driver by email, text or phone can’t move the load.

Can my team still reroute a load when plans change?

Yes. Your manager changes the drop in the command center. The reroute is signed, approved and sent to the driver.

What happens to the fake BOL or spoofed message?

It’s rejected and saved as evidence, including the sender and the message, and the sender is blocked across your fleet.

Does this replace GPS tracking?

No. Nihlus includes live GPS inside a geofenced corridor. Destination lock adds what a tracker can’t do: it stops the reroute before the truck moves.
