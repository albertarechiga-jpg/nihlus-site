The problem

Fewer trucks are being broken into. More loads are being stolen with a phone call or a fake bill of lading, and each one is worth far more. Tracking tells a shipper where the cargo went. It doesn’t stop it from going.

What Nihlus Vision does

One unbroken record, from the dock to the door. Each layer closes a gap that GPS tracking leaves open.

Verified pickup

Consent, iris, face and CDL on the driver’s phone, matched to the carrier, BOL, seal and trailer before the load is released.

Locked destination

Only the command center can change the drop. Spoofed emails and calls to the driver are blocked and saved as evidence.

Watching every mile

A geofenced corridor and a camera on the trailer door, with AI that spots a threat and goes live.

Response in seconds

Law-enforcement dispatch, an automatic evidence package and an insurance claim that fills itself in.
